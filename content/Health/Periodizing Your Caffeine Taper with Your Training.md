Most people under-estimate the energy and willpower gains you get from strength training and conditioning. 

When you do strength training, it's a good idea to periodize your training. This helps you make progress and avoid plateaus. A typical strength cycle is 1 month. Each week during the first 3 weeks in the month cycle the training gets harder and harder. The 4th week is for rest and recovery to cement the gains you made. 

You also periodize your cardio. This means your are building more muscle, and more cardio vascular health as the cycle progresses. And this is where the key lies. More muscle = more testosterone. More cardio = more energy capacity. 

Testosterone + Energy = Iron Will

If you are tapering caffeine, you are already minimizing the withdrawal effects. But the better shape you are in physically, the easier it is to get completely off caffeine. Because you have more energy and more testosterone. You have more willpower.

So each week during your training, your willpower and energy increases. So each week, you have the capacity to increase the amount of mg you are tapering by.
